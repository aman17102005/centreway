## Limits of this test

- Synthetic cards from my own generator; fonts are clean and layouts are ones I thought of.
- Hard photos use simple effects (blur, darkening, one shadow, tight crop); real phone photos have perspective, glare, folds and fingers.
- Hindi PDFs store text in scrambled order, so those are read by photo reading instead (the report shows "pdf-text+ocr").
- Centre positions are checked against my approximate coordinates, not surveyed ones.
- Timing is from a laptop; a low-end phone is several times slower.
